Letters

A computational installation about family memory, paper, distance, drifting, and return. This folder contains three interactive Three.js spatial canvases.

3D Canvas 01 · Three.js

Invisible Routes

Hundreds of paper fragments form a constellation. The cursor acts like gravity.

3D Canvas 02 · Three.js

Sea of Letters

A field of papers becomes a moving ocean surface.

3D Canvas 03 · Three.js

Archive Lantern

A warm lantern core pulls floating paper fragments into orbit.

3D Design Statement

These Three.js canvases turn memory into spatial fields. Paper becomes a constellation, an ocean, and a lantern. Gravity, waves, orbit, fog, and drifting replace conventional interface controls with an immersive spatial experience.

Some letters crossed the sea.
