RELATIONAL STRUCTURES IN MY WORK

From isolated actors to a system of power

Relational structures help me study algorithmic management as a socio-technical system rather than a single piece of software. Workers, customers, businesses, regulators, data, and automated decisions are represented as nodes; control, exchange, data collection, and collective action become edges.

The network makes unequal positions visible. The platform and its algorithms occupy highly connected positions, while workers are linked to income, time, safety, ratings, and data extraction. In a future project, I could compare this centralized network with a worker-owned platform cooperative to test how a change in ownership reorganizes decision-making and accountability.
